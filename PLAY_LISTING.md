## Full Description (<=4000 characters)

Unwind your mind and enjoy timeless card play with ZenSolitaire, a polished, tranquil take on classic Klondike Solitaire.

Crafted with care for peaceful focus and smooth responsiveness, ZenSolitaire blends modern felt aesthetics with harmonic procedural audio to deliver an effortless, calming flow state.

Whether you have a few minutes for a relaxing Draw 1 session or want to challenge your logic with a tactical Draw 3 deal, ZenSolitaire provides the ultimate offline patience card experience.

### 🌟 KEY FEATURES

• Draw 1 and Draw 3 Modes:
  - Draw 1: Smooth, forgiving, and deeply relaxing gameplay.
  - Draw 3: The authentic tournament challenge requiring foresight and strategy.

• Intuitive Touch & Drag Controls:
  - Smart single-tap automatically sends playable cards to their optimal foundation or tableau slot.
  - Natural drag-and-drop mechanics with floating card elevations and responsive touch tracking.
  - Double-tap or double-click to immediately build to foundations.

• Smart Hint System:
  - Stuck or looking for inspiration? Tap the lightbulb icon to see gentle, non-intrusive glowing suggestions for your next valid move.

• Automated Victory Cascade:
  - When all face-down cards are revealed, tap Auto-Finish to watch cards glide gracefully into their foundations with an animated victory celebration!

• Unlimited Multi-Step Undo:
  - Experiment with different moves risk-free. Revert moves or card draws at any time with complete peace of mind.

• Harmonious Procedural Web Audio:
  - Tactile card snaps, soft felt thuds, and melodic pentatonic bell chimes that rise in pitch as foundations are completed.
  - Built-in gentle haptics for physical feedback on mobile devices.
  - Instant one-tap sound toggle for quiet environments.

• Soothing Felt Themes:
  - Classic Emerald Green: The traditional casino felt look.
  - Midnight Velvet Navy: A deep, soothing dark mode.
  - Royal Indigo Purple: Regal, luxurious twilight felt.
  - Cherry Rose Wine: Warm, elegant mahogany-rose styling.

• Offline Statistics & Records:
  - Keep track of games played, win rate, fewest moves, best scores, fastest completion times, and current win streaks.

• 100% Offline & Private:
  - Perfect for flights, subway commutes, or off-grid relaxation.
  - Zero advertisements, no forced interruptions, no logins, and no data tracking.

Experience the relaxing satisfaction of cards in motion. Download ZenSolitaire today and discover your quiet puzzle sanctuary!
